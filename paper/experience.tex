\section{What the Deployment Cost}
\label{sec:experience}

This section reports the failures. They are not incidental to the contribution.
They are the part of it that cannot be obtained from a simulator, and most of
them are defects in measurement rather than in the system being measured.

\noindent\textbf{A telemetry vocabulary, and the limit of it.}
Every instrumented quantity in this system reports in a closed three-state
vocabulary rather than as a number. A value is measured, or converted with a
stated bound, or absent with a named reason. It is never a zero. Trigger
attribution is reported as fired, quiet, or not evaluable with the missing
inputs named, together with a reconstruction identity that ties the commanded
rate back to its base and scale. Field provenance is a closed eleven-member
vocabulary covering all 39 encoder slots, and logs recorded before that work
existed refuse by name rather than being scored against defaults. The session
summary reports answered and attempted as two independently counted integers.
There is no percentage anywhere in it and no scalar health field. A scalar is
what a dashboard plots on its own, and once it is plotted the enumeration is
gone.

That vocabulary has one limit. An axis of the form answered of attempted is
blind by construction to any event that stops the attempting. A 54.58\,s link outage destroyed the numerator and the denominator
together, removing about 273 of roughly 1{,}502 ticks, which is 18.2\% of every
denominator in that report. The summary recorded 1{,}229 attempts and called
every axis fully answered. The vocabulary protects the numerator, and nothing
was watching the denominator.

\noindent\textbf{What the failure inventory found before anything was built.}
An audit of both devices counted 186 failure conditions that the code already
detected. Four of them record when the failure happened. None records an
episode, meaning a second endpoint and an outcome. Several had no reader at all.
Two conditions were detected nowhere. The tick loop's no-frame branch counted
nothing, so a drive that was blind for 110 of 120\,s wrote the artefact of a
drive that was never blind. The worker used a try-finally block with no except
clause, so an exception ran teardown and wrote a summary that read like a clean
short run.

\noindent\textbf{The feature was inert on the one device it was written for.}
The two devices were in opposite states, and establishing that was the first
job. The phone's thermal chain was live end to end. The Jetson had no thermal
reading at all. The single sampler adjacent to it degraded to a silent no-op
when an optional import failed. It also wrote records that nothing collected,
and it had no test. Measured on the real device at the deployed commit, a 150\,s run produced
751 ticks, 0 sample records and 0 event records, with every tick reading absent
and the reason given as a stopped sampler. Three of that machine's nine thermal
zones answer with a temporary-unavailable error. That error surfaces through the
buffered text layer as a type error. The reader's handler covered operating
system errors only, so the sampler thread died on its first pass. The
fixtures could not have caught it. They make a zone unreadable by deleting the
file or by denying permission, and both of those raise the exception the reader
did catch. One sysfs quirk took the phone's thermal record down with the
Jetson's, on a drive where the phone was connected and delivering telemetry
throughout.

What prevented a wrong number was the reporting vocabulary. Nothing read quiet,
nothing read as a zero, and the report said outright that the drive answered
nothing. After the fix, a confirmation run on the same device recorded 1{,}200 ticks
and 241 samples, with 241 measured and 0 absent. The quiet line now carries the
evidence for its own claim, as 241 of 241 passes fully readable.

The field disagreed with the design estimate in three further ways. The device
has nine thermal zones, of which six are usable. It has 13 cooling devices where
the estimate assumed 3, which accounts for the entire 39\% overrun in record
size against per-item figures that were correct to a tenth of a byte. Thermal
headroom was not a number on 456 of 456 and on 297 of 297 reports, on a handset
whose thermal interface is connected and answering. A network redial erased a
drive's throttle count and wrote a phantom event, because the sampler copied the
phone's counter instead of accumulating it.

\noindent\textbf{An instrument that cancelled its own signal.}
The validator built to check the clock premise was degenerate, and algebra
caught it rather than a run. Estimating the offset on each clock pair and
differencing the two slopes cancels the quantity of interest and returns the
difference of the two local slews. It was confirmed rather than argued. It
reported $+12.06$\,ppm against an independently measured slew difference of
$+12.00$\,ppm. What works needs no network at all, because each device's own
wall-minus-monotonic slew is exact. The fitted estimate was $-1.09$\,ppm while
the true value was $-12.00$\,ppm, so charging the error bound to the fit's own
magnitude would have under-bounded it by a factor of ten.

\noindent\textbf{One defect hid every perception result in the project.}
The camera frames arrive rotated by ninety degrees, and that is why no drive in
this project had ever seen a vehicle. Measured over the full day, 16 of 22{,}929 ticks carried a detection, which is
0.070\%. The vehicle list was empty even on those 16, so zero distance estimates
were recorded on the entire day. There
were zero vehicle sightings across 4{,}151 ticks on one handset and 1{,}632 on
another. Table~\ref{tab:rotation} gives the measurement that identified it. The
frame shows a van filling much of the picture with a legible plate, so this was
not a marginal detection. Only the clockwise rotation recovers detections, and
anticlockwise and 180 degrees both give zero. The fix moves the intrinsics with
the rotation, taking the principal point from $(640, 360)$ to $(360, 640)$, and
the horizon was re-measured at 717\,px against the old landscape centre of 360.

This was one defect, and it had been recorded as several structural limits. An
infinite leader gap on every tick ever recorded, empty track lists everywhere,
and an always-empty local density are all downstream of it. The project had
accumulated explanations for an absence that had a single cause. The fix landed
after the collection day, so no drive in the corpus carries detections, and the
stored frames remain available for offline re-analysis.

\noindent\textbf{The vehicle is a hostile environment and the faults are
physical.}
Both devices ran from vehicle power for 152.8 minutes across eight runs with no
power interruption. Two install faults each cost drive time. Cable type is not
interchangeable, because one handset's cable failed in the vehicle, and the
application was moved to a second handset mid-session with the swap recorded
automatically in the run metadata. Direct sunlight on the dashboard heated the
tethering handset enough that its hotspot shut off, which ended a drive. The
mount needs shade and not only a clamp.

\noindent\textbf{Two method conclusions.}
The first is that a fixture's failure mode has to be the field's. A deleted file
and a denied permission raise one exception, the real device raised a different
one, and every test passed while the feature did nothing. The second is
quantitative. On the thermal task alone, five distinct false readings were
produced by measurement harnesses rather than by the code under test.

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\noindent\textbf{Holding a bottleneck below breakdown.} Ramp metering~\cite{papageorgiou2002freeway} and variable speed limits~\cite{hegyi2008specialist} limit the flow that reaches a bottleneck, observing it through loop detectors and acting through roadside signals. Adding floating-car probe data and traffic camera video to such a scheme shortened jam detection and improved its speed limits in simulation~\cite{hegyi2013cooperative}, so a controller that learns of a jam sooner controls it better. Our system pursues the same goal without roadside equipment: the vehicles both sense and act.

\noindent\textbf{Vehicles as actuators.} Prior work has shown that a single controlled vehicle damps stop-and-go waves on a ring road~\cite{stern2018dissipation}, and reinforcement learning controllers have been shown to work for mixed-autonomy traffic~\cite{wu2017flow}. Our controller lies in the same vein, except the traffic state is not given but is obtained from a commercial feed and the vehicle's own camera. Earlier work has also established that coarse-grained advisories can steer human drivers~\cite{cho2023temporal}, and the CIRCLES project deployed about one hundred vehicles on an interstate under a centralized speed planner~\cite{lee2024traffic}. These studies motivate our choice of a speed advisory design over alternatives. Telematics applications already show drivers feedback on their phones, hence, our system advises through a phone and leaves the vehicle's controls to the driver.

\noindent\textbf{Probe data and coordination without messages.} For connected vehicles to share their own measurements over V2X, most vehicles would need to carry radios. The commercial probe feeds report a speed change minutes late~\cite{wang2018latency}. To keep the decentralized design, our vehicles coordinate through common information~\cite{nayyar2013common}, each reads the same feed version and computes the same plan from it. To mitigate the effects of lag, we use the dashboard camera to add a correction to the advisory.
